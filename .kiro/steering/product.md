# Product steering — AWS Practical Skills Lab

## Mission

Build practical AWS application-development competence first, with DVA-C03 preparation as a secondary outcome. Teach through realistic implementation, troubleshooting, evidence, and learner explanation rather than exam drills.

## Learner

The learner is an experienced developer in JavaScript, TypeScript, and Python, already familiar with REST, databases, Docker, and Git. Spend time on AWS-specific service behavior, IAM, deployment, reliability, security, and operations.

## Learning experience

- Work on one active competency/lab at a time; honor the progression gate in `CURRICULUM.md`.
- Distinguish guided implementation from independent, troubleshooting, and optimization levels.
- Preserve learner agency: assignments are not solved on the learner's behalf.
- Assess both observable behavior and the learner's reasoning.
- Preserve the learner's own reflection text in `LEARNING.md`.
